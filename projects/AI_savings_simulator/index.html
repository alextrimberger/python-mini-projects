How this works

The model takes one routine back-office task and costs it three ways for a year. People only: staff do every task and fix their own errors. With AI, the AI attempts the share of tasks it can cover. An attempt goes straight through only if every step succeeds. Each step can fail through AI error or through process friction, such as missing data or an unclear hand-off. Failed attempts become exceptions that a person handles, at extra effort. A sample of the AI's completed work is spot-checked. Friction also makes each AI attempt costlier through retries. Fixing the foundations has a one-off cost, spread over the years you choose.

  • Costs are annual and in today's money, with no inflation or discounting.
  • People can be hired in fractions. FTE is not rounded to whole people.
  • Hires each year cover staff turnover plus all the extra people needed to reach the selected volume, counted in a single year.
  • Every step has the same friction and the same AI reliability.
  • Exceptions and spot checks are assumed to be error-free.
  • The AI platform cost is fixed and doesn't grow with volume.
  • Fixing the foundations is assumed not to change the speed or error rate of people-only work.
